Pit sandwiches & barbecue

Charcoal Grill is a neighborhood restaurant on Old Harford Road in Parkville, specializing in pit sandwiches and barbecue.

What we cook

{{photo-wings}}

  • Ribs and chicken

    Baby back ribs, regular or hickory BBQ, plus half chicken and wings.

  • Ocean Fries

    Fresh-cut and cooked in peanut oil.
