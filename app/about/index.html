About Charcoal Grill

Charcoal Grill is a neighborhood restaurant on Old Harford Road in Parkville, Maryland, specializing in pit sandwiches and barbecue. Order online for pickup, call ahead, or arrange catering for a crowd.

{{photo-wings}}

What we cook

Pit beef
Prepared to your preference, regular or colossal.
Ham, turkey and Italian sausage
Barbecued ham, smoked and grilled turkey, and Roma Italian sausage with peppers and onions.
Ribs, chicken and wings
Baby back ribs, regular or hickory BBQ, plus half chicken and wings with celery and ranch or bleu cheese.
Ocean Fries
Fresh-cut and cooked in peanut oil.
