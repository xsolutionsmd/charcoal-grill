Our menu

Pit sandwiches are the heart of it: pit beef, barbecued ham, smoked and grilled turkey and Roma Italian sausage with peppers and onions, regular or colossal.

Alongside them you will find baby back ribs and wings, Caesar salads and soups, junior meals, Ocean Fries cooked in peanut oil, and fried dough to finish.
