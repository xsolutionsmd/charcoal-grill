## Matching limits

The Buffalo Items image depicts wings, not every boneless/shrimp option. The soup and dessert group-card images visually match chicken noodle soup and funnel cake fries; these are observed dish matches, not separately photographed variants. Rib photographs illustrate the item rather than guaranteeing pictured portion size. No photos are assigned to unpictured variants.

Other collected images remain available in the private library. Ingredient packaging, supplier/drink logos, mixed-dish collages, illustrated kids meals, a visibly mismatched cordon-bleu presentation, and images for items outside the existing menu were not placed as individual dish photos. Missing pictures do not create placeholder tiles or invent menu content.

ChowNow provided the usable matches. The exact Uber Eats listing exposes no menu because it is closed on that platform; bounded DoorDash searches did not establish a matching store. These are source-access/identity limits, not asset-permission restrictions.
